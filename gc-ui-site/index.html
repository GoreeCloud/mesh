Separation of authority

Connected, not centralized.

Each Integral Platform System retains its distinct security, privacy, identity and continuity responsibilities.

Identity

Authenticates principals, devices, credentials, sessions and delegated authority.

Wardveil Security

Owns protection, threat detection, security verification, response and evidence.

Privacy Shield

Owns consent, minimization, retention boundaries and user privacy controls.

Everkeep

Owns preservation, recovery, portability, backup and continuity.
